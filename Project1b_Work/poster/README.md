# Project 1b poster workspace

The poster is a separate one-page PDF. It must agree with the report and sell
the project in about 15 seconds. Include:

- Group number and member names
- Mission statement: why, what, and measurable so what
- Stakeholders
- Repository, discussion-forum, and feature walk-through video URLs or QR codes
- Tech-stack icons and small screenshots
- Three to five Before, Now, and Future milestones per group
- A concise boast supported by the Project 1a test evidence

Keep source assets in `assets/`. Confirm the final page is letter or A4, has no
large empty areas, and remains readable when printed.
