## Codex-only interpretation

- All ten candidates have live supporting URLs, so each passes the literal
  one-model-plus-live-URL rule. The final team list should still prefer
  products independently named by another model.
- WICShopper, Bnft, myWIC Mosaic, and ebtEDGE are the strongest platform-level
  comparisons. The state-branded apps are close user-workflow comparisons but
  may share underlying vendors or infrastructure.
- No market-gap claim is validated yet. Missing features must be checked on
  deeper product/support pages rather than inferred from short app summaries.
